This Cookie Policy explains how VoiceGab ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website at voicegab.com (the "Website"). It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and pixels. They can be used to make websites work, remember your preferences, or track your activity across sites.

2. What We Use on This Website

Our Website is a static, informational site. We keep its footprint deliberately small:

  • No advertising cookies. We do not serve ads and do not use any advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • No analytics cookies. The Website does not run analytics scripts that set cookies on your device.
  • Strictly necessary storage only. The Website uses browser local storage for basic functionality. This data stays in your browser and is never transmitted to us.

The only item we currently store is:

  • vg-cookie-consent (local storage, strictly necessary) — remembers which cookie categories you allowed in our consent banner, and when, so we don't ask you on every page. It expires after 12 months, after which we ask again.

Your choice: When you first visit, a banner asks for your consent. You can choose Accept All, Reject All, or Customize to switch individual categories on or off:

  • Strictly necessary — always active; required for the Website to work (for example, remembering your cookie choice).
  • Analytics — off unless you allow it. Would help us understand how visitors use the Website. Not currently in use.
  • Marketing — off unless you allow it. Would be used to measure campaigns and show relevant content. Not currently in use.

We do not run any analytics or marketing cookies today. If we add any in the future, they will only be activated for visitors who have allowed that category, and we will update this policy to list them first. Rejecting optional cookies has no effect on how the Website works.

3. Third-Party Services

The Website loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these fonts, it sends standard technical data (such as your IP address and browser type) to Google's servers. Google states that font requests are not used for tracking and are cached. You can learn more in Google Fonts' privacy FAQ.

The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard technical data (such as IP addresses) for security and operational purposes, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

4. Cookies and the Mobile App

The VoiceGab mobile application does not use browser cookies. For information about the data the app collects and how it is used, please see our Privacy Policy.

5. Managing Cookies

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by clicking Cookie Settings in the footer of any page, or using the button below.

You can also control and delete cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block all cookies, block third-party cookies, or clear stored data when you close the browser. Because our Website uses only strictly necessary storage, blocking cookies will not meaningfully affect your experience here.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time, for example if we add new functionality to the Website. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised. Material changes will be announced on the Website.

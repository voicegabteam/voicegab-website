FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Open a conversation, press and hold the mic button at the bottom of the screen, speak your message, then release to send. You can also tap the mic button once to start recording and tap again to stop — great for longer messages.

After recording a voice message, tap the filter icon to browse available voice filters. You can preview how each filter sounds before sending. Choose from Robot, Echo, Chipmunk, Deep Voice, Chorus, Underwater, and more. Filters are applied locally on your device using AI processing.

Yes. All private voice messages are end-to-end encrypted using AES-256-GCM. This means your messages are encrypted on your device before being sent, and can only be decrypted by the intended recipient. Not even VoiceGab can access your message content.

Long-press on any message you've sent to reveal the delete option. You can choose to delete the message for yourself only, or delete for everyone in the conversation. Deleted messages are permanently removed from our servers.

Yes! VoiceGab uses a local-first database architecture. You can browse your existing conversations, listen to downloaded messages, and even record new voice messages while offline. All queued messages will be sent automatically when your connection is restored.

After recording, you'll see a preview screen with the filter selector. You can tap different filters to hear how your recording sounds with each one applied. The filter is applied in real-time, so you can compare before choosing. Once you send, the filter cannot be changed.

VoiceGab is lightweight by design. The app itself is under 50MB. Voice messages use the Opus codec at 16kbps, which means a 1-minute message is only about 120KB. Your message history is stored locally in an encrypted SQLite database. You can manage storage in Settings > Storage.

Open the user's profile by tapping their name in a conversation, then tap the three-dot menu and select "Report User." You can choose a reason for the report and add optional details. Our moderation team reviews all reports within 24 hours.

Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. You'll be asked to confirm. Account deletion is permanent — all your data, including conversation history, voice messages, and account information will be permanently erased from our servers within 30 days.

You can reach us at support@voicegab.com. You can also use the in-app feedback option in Settings > Help & Feedback. Our support team responds within 24 hours.

End-to-End Encryption

Military-grade security, by default.

Every voice message is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it leaves your device. Your local database is protected with SQLCipher using 256K PBKDF2 iterations. We can't read your messages, and neither can anyone else.

  • AES-256-GCM encryption at rest and in transit
  • Certificate-pinned HTTPS with TLS 1.3
  • HMAC-SHA256 signed API requests
